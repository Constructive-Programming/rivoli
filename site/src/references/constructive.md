# Constructive

[Constructive](https://www.constructive.dev/) is the consultancy behind [rivoli](../index.md). It works with platform teams on
type-driven architecture: fewer representable states, fewer bugs, and claims that are
backed by a check that can fail.

It offers architecture reviews, embedded senior engineers in [Scala](https://www.scala-lang.org/),
[Rust](https://www.rust-lang.org/) and [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/),
agent-native development workflows, and workshops.

rivoli makes the same argument the consultancy makes in its engagements. Put the rules in
the types and the crate graph, and whole classes of wrong programs stop compiling. Then
prove every remaining claim with a gate that has been seen to go red.
